Just This privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Just This is an Android app made by Scorpius Labs. It lets you hand your phone to someone and show only the photos and videos (or the part of one) you choose. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Your photos and videos never leave your phone

You pick photos and videos with Android's system photo picker, or share them into the app from another app. The app only gets access to the items you select, displays or plays them on your screen, and never uploads, copies to a server, or shares them. We do not run servers for the app and do not ask for an account.

Your settings (auto-lock time, Pro unlock time, owner PIN and snoop message) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. The snoop counter is shown to you after each session and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home and summary screens, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to unlock Pro for 24 hours. Ads are never shown while your photos or videos are on screen.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • IP address, used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (added by Google's ads SDK): to show and measure ads.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional, only if you turn on "Mute notifications"): to pause notifications while you are showing photos or videos. Your previous setting is restored afterwards.
  • Screen pinning (optional "Pin screen"): keeps the person you hand your phone to inside the viewer. You confirm it on screen.

The app does not request access to your whole photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

Just This is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
